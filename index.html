01 — Studio

A small studio with a big standard for play.

PRIME LEAGUE LLC is a California limited liability company that designs, develops, and publishes interactive entertainment. We focus on original ideas, tight game feel, and polish you can notice in every second of play.

Craft first

We iterate until the controls feel right and every system earns its place. Quality over quantity, always.

Player-respecting

Fair design, clear pricing, and no dark patterns. We build the kind of games we want to play ourselves.

Built to last

We support what we ship — with updates, fixes, and direct, responsive support from the team that made it.
