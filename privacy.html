This Privacy Policy describes how PRIME LEAGUE LLC (“PRIME LEAGUE LLC,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with our website, games, and applications (together, the “Services”). We are an independent game development studio based in Arcadia, California, United States.

1. Who we are

The data controller for the Services is:

PRIME LEAGUE LLC
Arcadia, California
United States
Email: info@primeleague.net

2. Information we may collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the Services. We do not use advertising trackers on this website. We may collect:

  • Information you provide. For example, your name, email address, and the content of a message if you contact us.
  • Purchase and account information. If you buy or download our games or apps through a storefront such as Steam or the Apple App Store, the platform processes the payment. We may receive limited information needed to fulfill the purchase or provide support, such as a platform account identifier, purchase confirmation, and country or region. We do not receive your full payment card number from these storefronts.
  • Technical and device information. Such as device type, operating system, language, approximate region, crash or diagnostic logs, and similar data that helps us operate and improve the Services.
  • Gameplay and usage information. If a game or app includes progress, settings, or similar features, we may store data needed to provide those features.

This website is a static site and does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser may still send standard technical information (such as IP address and user agent) to the party that hosts the site, as is typical for any website visit.

3. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Services;
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support;
  • Fulfill purchases and provide updates or related content;
  • Monitor and fix errors, security issues, and fraud;
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms; and
  • Communicate with you about our games and Services when you have asked us to, or as otherwise permitted by law.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us host the website, send email, store data, or analyze technical issues, under contracts that limit their use of the information;
  • Platform operators such as Valve (Steam) and Apple, to the extent needed to distribute, update, or support a game or app;
  • Professional advisors such as lawyers or accountants, when reasonably necessary; and
  • Authorities or other parties when we believe disclosure is required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a corporate transaction such as a merger or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law (for example, to resolve disputes or meet tax or accounting obligations).

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website. If a specific game or app is intended for a different age group, that product may include additional notices. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to appeal a decision we make about a request. California residents may also have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

To make a request, contact us at info@primeleague.net. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows it.

9. International visitors

We are located in the United States. If you access the Services from another country, your information may be processed in the United States or other locations where we or our service providers operate, which may have data-protection rules that differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice as required by law.
